How to use the agent.

Start small, connect authorized data sources, validate the intelligence, then let the workforce layer turn recurring customer signals into continuous improvement.

Connect your business

Provide business context and, when available, connect reviews, surveys, support, CRM or other authorized feedback sources.

Define the outcome

Choose what matters: retention, satisfaction, product quality, service recovery, reputation or growth.

Analyze the voice

The agent organizes feedback into themes, sentiment, intent, evidence, risks and opportunities.

Validate intelligence

Review confidence, evidence, unknowns and assumptions before taking consequential action.

Turn insight into action

Create tasks, experiments, follow-ups, service improvements or handoffs to other OrionPulse agents.

Measure and learn

Track whether changes improve customer experience and business KPIs, then feed results back into the system.
